Regular Plans vs Direct Plans

Mutual funds in India offer two purchase options under SEBI regulations. Choose the one that suits your investing style.

Regular Plan

Invest Through a Distributor

Enrolled through an AMFI-registered distributor (such as Vikas Agarwal). We provide goal discovery, scheme matching, KYC assistance, execution through CAMS/KFintech, portfolio reviews, and rebalancing.

Point-of-Sale Disclosure

Commission & Remuneration Policy

In strict compliance with SEBI and AMFI regulations, all upfront commissions on mutual funds are 0.00% (strictly prohibited). Vikas Agarwal receives purely ongoing trail commissions paid directly by Asset Management Companies (AMCs) out of the scheme's Total Expense Ratio (TER).

SEBI Point-of-Sale Commission Disclosure Norm

Commission rates vary across Asset Management Companies, scheme categories (Equity, Hybrid, Debt, Liquid), and regulatory Total Expense Ratio (TER) slabs. In accordance with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/IMD/CIR No.4/168230/09, the exact commission details of any scheme recommended to you, along with comparisons to competing schemes, are provided directly to you at the point of sale prior to executing any transaction.
