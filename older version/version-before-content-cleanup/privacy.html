Data Privacy & Protection

Privacy Policy & Client Data Safeguards

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect when you invest in mutual funds through Vikas Agarwal (ARN-40434), how it is safeguarded under Indian data protection laws, and your rights as an investor.

Effective Date:January 2026
Governing Law:DPDP Act & SEBI Regulations
Data Principal:You retain full rights
Data Collection

Information We Collect

To facilitate statutory Know Your Customer (KYC) compliance and process mutual fund investments under SEBI/AMFI regulations, we collect the following information:

🪪

Identity & KYC Data

Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, PAN (Permanent Account Number), and Aadhaar details (collected strictly via regulated offline/e-KYC modes).

📞

Contact Data

Email address, mobile telephone number, and residential/office address.

🏦

Financial & Banking Data

Bank account number, IFSC code, bank branch, and account type (required to set up SIP auto-debit mandates and receive redemption credits directly).

📊

Investment Profile

Investment goals, time horizon, and risk tolerance profile.

Usage

How Your Information is Used

Your information is used strictly for legitimate financial transaction purposes:

1

KYC Registration

Registering and updating your KYC status with SEBI-registered KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs: CAMS KRA, NDML, CVL, Karvy).

2

Transaction Processing

Submitting mutual fund purchase, SIP, redemption, switch, and nomination requests with respective Asset Management Companies (AMCs).

3

Portfolio Servicing

Providing periodic portfolio valuation summaries and goal tracking reviews.

4

Statutory Communication

Communicating statutory transaction confirmations, account statements, and tax reports.

Zero-Spam & Zero-Selling Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or financial data to any third-party marketing companies, telemarketers, or loan providers under any circumstances.

Data Sharing

Authorized Data Sharing with Regulated Infrastructure

To execute your mutual fund transactions, your information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with regulated entities:

Mutual Fund AMCs
Registrars & RTAs (CAMS, KFintech)
MF Utility (MFU), IFA Express
BSE StAR MF / NSE NMF II
SEBI, AMFI, FIU-IND
Income Tax Authorities

Sharing is strictly for transaction execution, folio maintenance, statement generation, and statutory compliance — never for marketing.

Security

Data Storage & Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Client documents and KYC records are stored in access-controlled, password-protected environments.
  • Transmission of information via digital channels is handled with industry-standard encryption protocols.
  • Paper copies, where collected for physical signature verification, are securely archived in accordance with SEBI record retention guidelines.
Your Rights

Investor Rights under the DPDP Act

As a data principal, you have the right to:

🔍

Access

Access a summary of your personal information held with us.

✏️

Correction

Request correction or updating of inaccurate or outdated contact/banking information.

🚫

Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for optional communications (statutory mutual fund statements continue as mandated by SEBI).
