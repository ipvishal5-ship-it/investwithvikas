Eligibility

Investor Eligibility & Age of Majority (18+ Requirement)

Statutory requirements governing mutual fund investments under Indian law and SEBI regulations.

  • Age of Majority: Under the Indian Majority Act, 1875 and SEBI KYC regulations, investors must be at least 18 years of age to enter into binding financial contracts and execute mutual fund investments.
  • Investments by Minors: Individuals under 18 may invest solely through a parent or court-appointed legal guardian in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2019/166. Minor folios require submission of valid proof of date of birth and documentation establishing the guardian's relationship.
  • Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) & Foreign Compliance: NRIs and PIOs are eligible to invest in Indian mutual funds under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) through verified NRE or NRO bank accounts. Due to FATCA/CRS and US SEC restrictions, certain AMCs restrict investments from residents of the United States and Canada.
  • Governing Law & Jurisdiction: All mutual fund distribution relationships and website usage are governed exclusively by the laws of the Republic of India, with exclusive jurisdiction in the competent courts of Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

Investor Grievance Redressal

Under SEBI guidelines, all investors have access to a transparent, structured multi-tier dispute redressal mechanism. Escalate in the order shown below.

1

Level 1 — Distributor Direct Redressal

For service issues, transaction delays, statement queries, or complaints regarding mutual fund distribution, reach out directly to the distributor.

2

Level 2 — Asset Management Company (AMC) / RTA

If your grievance pertains to scheme NAV, dividend credit, redemption payout, or folio data, lodge a complaint directly with the Investor Relations Officer of the concerned mutual fund house or Registrar (CAMS / KFintech).

3

Level 3 — SEBI SCORES 2.0

If your complaint is not resolved, or you are not satisfied with the response from the distributor or AMC, you can lodge a complaint with SEBI on the SCORES 2.0 portal. Under SCORES 2.0, the entity concerned has 21 calendar days to respond.

SCORES Portal: scores.sebi.gov.in
SEBI Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 266 7575 / 1800 22 7575 (toll-free; see SEBI’s website for current hours and languages)

4

Level 4 — SMART ODR Portal

SEBI has established an independent online dispute resolution mechanism (SMART ODR) for the securities market. If you are not satisfied with the SCORES outcome, you can initiate online conciliation or arbitration.

SMART ODR Portal: smartodr.in
