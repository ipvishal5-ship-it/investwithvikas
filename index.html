What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan lets you invest a fixed amount at regular intervals, usually every month.

How much do I need to start?

Many schemes let you start with a small monthly amount. We will decide an amount that fits your budget and your goal.

What is ELSS, and what is the lock-in?

ELSS is a tax-saving equity fund. Each investment is locked in for 3 years. The Section 80C deduction applies under the old tax regime.

Are mutual funds safe?

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, and values can go up and down. We choose by your goal and your comfort with risk.

How do you earn?

I distribute Regular Plans. The fund house pays me a commission, which is part of the scheme’s expense ratio. See full details.

What do I need to start?

KYC documents (such as PAN and Aadhaar) and your bank details. I will guide you through it.
