Policy

Commission Disclosures

Investments facilitated through Vikas Agarwal are executed in Regular Plans, where trail commission is received from respective Asset Management Companies (AMCs) within the scheme’s expense ratio.

Official Plan & Remuneration Disclosure

Commission details for any recommended scheme and its competing peer schemes are disclosed at the point of sale and remain available anytime upon request. All remuneration is paid by the fund house.

Plan Types

Regular Plans vs Direct Plans

Mutual funds can be bought as Regular Plans (through a distributor) or as Direct Plans.

SEBI Direct Plan Notice

Investors have the option to invest in Direct Plans of mutual fund schemes directly, without intermediary commission, through AMC websites, registrar portals (CAMS, KFintech), or MF Central.

Point-of-Sale Disclosure

Commission Disclosures

SEBI regulations prohibit upfront commissions on mutual funds. Remuneration is received strictly as ongoing trail commission, paid by AMCs out of the scheme’s expense ratio.

SEBI Point-of-Sale Commission Disclosure Norm

Commission rates vary across Asset Management Companies, scheme categories (Equity, Hybrid, Debt, Liquid) and expense ratio slabs. The commission schedule for any proposed scheme is shared with investors prior to transaction execution.

Fund houses also publish commission details

Fund houses disclose the commission they pay to distributors on their own websites. You can check the website of the fund house concerned (listed below) for these details.
