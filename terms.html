2. Investor Eligibility & Age of Majority (18+ Requirement)

Access to mutual fund transaction facilities and distribution services is subject to statutory investor eligibility rules:

  • Age of Majority: Investors must be at least 18 years of age (under the Indian Majority Act, 1875) and legally competent to enter into binding financial contracts.
  • Minors Investing: Individuals under 18 years of age may invest solely through a parent or court-appointed legal guardian in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2019/166. Minor investments require valid proof of age, date of birth, and documentation proving the guardian's relationship.
  • KYC Compliance: Every prospective investor must hold a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) and be KYC-compliant (Validated status across CVL, CAMS, KFintech, NSE, or NDML KRA) before executing any transaction.

3. Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) & Foreign Jurisdictions

This website and services are designed primarily for Indian citizens, Persons of Indian Origin (PIO), and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) eligible to invest in Indian mutual funds under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA):

  • Permitted Accounts: NRI investments must originate from verified NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank accounts.
  • US & Canada Restrictions: Due to stringent foreign tax regulations (FATCA, CRS, and US SEC rules), certain Indian AMCs do not accept investments from residents of the United States and Canada. NRI investors are responsible for determining whether their resident jurisdiction permits investments in Indian securities.

4. Distribution Scope & Remuneration

Vikas Agarwal distributes Regular Plans of mutual funds empaneled with registered Asset Management Companies (AMCs). He receives trail commissions directly from the fund houses, paid out of the scheme's Total Expense Ratio (TER), as disclosed on our Commission Disclosures page. No direct advisory or handling fees are charged to the client.

Investors always have the option to invest in Direct Plans offered by mutual funds directly, which carry lower expense ratios due to the absence of distributor commissions.
