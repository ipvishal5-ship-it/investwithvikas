The Compounding Formula Behind SIP

A Systematic Investment Plan works on the Future Value of an Annuity Due model. Because investments are made at the beginning of every month, every monthly contribution earns compound interest over its own tenure.

FV = P × [((1 + r)^n - 1) / r] × (1 + r)
  • P: Fixed Monthly Investment Installment
  • r: Monthly Rate of Return (Annual Return ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n: Total Number of Months (Tenure in Years × 12)

Why Step-Up SIP Outperforms Regular SIP

A standard SIP assumes your investment capacity never changes. A Step-Up SIP automatically raises your investment by 5% or 10% each year in line with salary increments.

Because the largest contributions enter during your peak earning years and compound on top of the established base, a 10% annual step-up typically yields 60% to 80% higher final wealth than a fixed SIP.
