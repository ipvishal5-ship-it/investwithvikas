COMPOUNDING BLUEPRINT

How Beginning-of-Month SIPs Build Wealth

Unlike standard bank deposits credited at term-end, a mutual fund Systematic Investment Plan functions as an Annuity Due model. Because each installment is invested at the beginning of the monthly billing cycle, your capital starts participating in market movements from Day 1.

FV = P ×
(1 + r)n − 1 r
× (1 + r)
Annuity Due
  • P: Fixed monthly commitment deposited at the start of each month
  • r: Periodic monthly return derived from expected annual portfolio growth
  • n: Total monthly compounding cycles across your goal horizon (Years × 12)
  • (1 + r): The Day-1 multiplier ensuring your initial month starts compounding immediately
INVESTING RULE OF THUMB

Why Discipline Beats Market Timing

Attempting to time market highs and lows to chase an extra 1%–2% return adds significant emotional anxiety. A disciplined 10% Annual Step-Up SIP—scaling contributions alongside career appraisals—helps build long-term wealth. Starting earlier gives compounding more time to work. Actual results depend on market returns.

Three Pillars of Compounding Success

  • Counters Lifestyle Inflation: Automatically redirects increments into assets before spending leaks.
  • Heavyweight Senior Years: Deploys your largest contributions during peak earning decades.
  • Starting 3 Years Early: Saves up to 35% in required out-of-pocket investment capital.
