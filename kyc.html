100% DIGITAL ONBOARDING

The 5-Minute Paperless e-KYC Process

New investors can complete verification completely online via Aadhaar OTP and DigiLocker without submitting physical photocopies or visiting a branch.

01

PAN & Demographics

Provide your 10-digit PAN and Date of Birth. The system cross-verifies your name against Income Tax Department (ITD) records in real time.

02

Aadhaar via DigiLocker

Authenticate through Government DigiLocker or UIDAI Aadhaar OTP. Your officially valid document (OVD) is validated immediately.

03

Penny-Drop Bank Check

Enter your IFSC and Bank Account Number. A ₹1 micro-deposit confirms account holder name matching before your SIP mandate activates.

04

5-Sec Video IPV

A fast 5-second In-Person Verification (IPV) selfie video verifies liveness as required by SEBI anti-money laundering mandates.
