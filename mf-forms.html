Transaction Universal · 1 Pg

Common Transaction Slip (Purchase / Switch / Redemption)

Standard universal transaction slip for executing additional investments, switching units between mutual fund schemes, or initiating partial/full redemptions across any SEBI-registered fund house.

Generic template, not an official AMC or RTA form. Your fund house or RTA may require its own form.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Existing Folio Number and Scheme / Plan / Option
  • Bank cheque leaf or pay-in slip for additional purchase
  • Signatures of all joint unitholders (as per folio holding mode)

Comprehensive multi-fund transaction format for investor service centers covering Nippon India, Mirae Asset, UTI, Axis, and other KFintech-serviced mutual fund houses.

Key Submission Requirements

  • Self-attested PAN card copy of primary holder
  • Pre-printed account holder cheque for new purchases

KYC Registry 620 KB · 4 Pgs

Central KYC (CKYC) Application Form · Individual

Standardized application for individual resident Indians and NRI investors to create or update their 14-digit Central KYC Identification Number (KIN) with CERSAI.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Recent passport-size photograph with signature across
  • Self-attested PAN card copy and Officially Valid Document (OVD)
  • In-Person Verification (IPV) stamp by registered MFD or AMC

Non-Individual (Corporate & Entity) CKYC Form

Uniform KYC application for corporate bodies, Private Limited companies, Partnership firms, LLPs, Trusts, Societies, and HUFs investing in mutual fund folios.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Certificate of Incorporation, MOA & AOA or Partnership Deed
  • Board Resolution / Authority Letter with Authorized Signatory List
  • Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) declaration & Annexure

FATCA & CRS Self-Certification Declaration

Mandatory regulatory self-certification under the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act and Common Reporting Standard for declaring tax residency in or outside India.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Country of Tax Residence and Place of Birth
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN) or functional equivalent
  • Signature of individual investor or authorized entity signatory

Minor to Major 554 KB · 3 Pgs

Minor Attaining Majority (MAM) Request Form

Uniform AMFI/SEBI application to convert mutual fund folio status from minor to major when the child reaches 18 years of age, transferring full independent operational control.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Self-attested PAN card copy and fresh KYC acknowledgment of the major
  • Cancelled cheque leaf of new bank account in the sole name of the major
  • Signature attestation by registered guardian, bank manager, or notary

Banking & Auto-Debit 196 KB · 1 Pg

One-Time Mandate (OTM) / NACH Registration Form

National Automated Clearing House (NACH) mandate slip to register automated SIP debits directly from your savings or current bank account across mutual fund folios.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Bank Account Number, Bank Name, Branch, and 11-digit IFSC
  • Exact signature matching bank branch signature records
  • Specified maximum daily debit limit (typically Rs 1 Lakh or 5 Lakh)

Bank Change Universal · 1 Pg

Change of Bank Mandate & Multiple Banks Registration

Used to modify your registered payout bank account or register up to 5 individual bank accounts per folio across any mutual fund for seamless payout routing and core-banking IFSC migration.

Generic template, not an official AMC or RTA form. Your fund house or RTA may require its own form.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Original cancelled cheque of new bank with pre-printed investor name
  • Proof of existing/old bank account (cancelled cheque or passbook copy)
  • Signatures of all joint unitholders as per folio records

Nomination Registration, Modification & Cancellation

Official SEBI/AMFI standardized form to register up to three nominees with percentage allocation, modify existing nominees, or submit formal opt-out declaration.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Full Name, PAN / Aadhaar / Relationship of each Nominee
  • Guardian details & proof of date of birth if nominee is a minor
  • Mandatory signatures of ALL folio unitholders

Mutual Fund Transmission Form · Form T3 (Registered Nominee)

Standardized application for transmitting mutual fund units to the registered nominee upon the unfortunate demise of the sole unitholder or all joint holders.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Original Death Certificate or copy duly attested by Gazetted Officer/Notary
  • Completed KYC of the claimant/nominee (Aadhaar, PAN)
  • Cancelled cheque of nominee's bank account with pre-printed name

Transmission (Legal Heir) 178 KB · 3 Pgs

Transmission Form · Form T1 (Where No Nominee is Registered)

Application for legal heirs to claim transmission of mutual fund units where the deceased unitholder did not register a nominee, within statutory threshold limits.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Attested Death Certificate and Legal Heirship Certificate / Succession Deed
  • Indemnity Bond and No Objection Certificate (NOC) from all legal heirs
  • KYC of claimant legal heir with bank account proofs

Transmission Indemnity Bond, Affidavits & NOC (Form 3)

Standardized legal formats required by mutual fund registrars: Non-judicial stamp paper affidavit, Indemnity Bond, and No Objection Certificates for legal heir settlements.

Key Submission Requirements
  • Execution on appropriate non-judicial Stamp Paper (as per state stamp duty)
  • Notarization by an authorized Notary Public with registration seal
