Data Privacy & Protection

Privacy Policy & Client Data Safeguards

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect when you invest in mutual funds through Vikas Agarwal (ARN-40434), how it is safeguarded in line with Indian data protection laws, and your rights as an investor.

Effective Date:November 2026
Framework:In line with the DPDP Act & SEBI Regulations
Your rights:Access, correction, erasure, grievance, nomination
Data Collection

Information We Collect

To facilitate statutory Know Your Customer (KYC) compliance and process mutual fund investments under SEBI/AMFI regulations, and to send you statements and statutory communication, we collect the following information:

Identity & KYC Data

Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, PAN (Permanent Account Number), and Aadhaar details (collected strictly via regulated offline/e-KYC modes).

Contact Data

Email address, mobile telephone number, and residential/office address.

Financial & Banking Data

Bank account number, IFSC code, bank branch, and account type (required to set up SIP auto-debit mandates and receive redemption credits directly).

Investment Profile

Investment goals, time horizon, and risk tolerance profile.

No selling of your data

We do not sell or rent your personal or financial data to marketers or other third parties.

Data Sharing

Authorized Data Sharing with Regulated Infrastructure

To execute your mutual fund transactions, your information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with regulated entities:

Mutual Fund AMCs
Registrars & RTAs (CAMS, KFintech)
MF Utility (MFU)
BSE StAR MF / NSE NMF II

Sharing is strictly for transaction execution, folio maintenance, statement generation, and statutory compliance — never for marketing.

Security

Data Storage, Retention & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Paper copies, where collected for physical signature verification, are securely archived in accordance with SEBI record retention guidelines.
  • Retention: We keep your records while you remain a client, and afterwards only for the period required by SEBI, AMFI, KYC and tax rules, after which they are securely deleted or destroyed.
  • This website: Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers (which can see your IP address) when you open a page. This site does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no forms that collect your data. If you contact us by WhatsApp, call or email, that service’s own terms apply to the message.
Your Rights

Your Rights as a Data Principal

In line with the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

Access

Access a summary of your personal information held with us.

Correction

Request correction or updating of inaccurate or outdated contact/banking information.

Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for optional communications (statutory mutual fund statements continue as mandated by SEBI).

Erasure

Ask us to erase your personal data. We may need to keep records that SEBI, AMFI, KYC or tax rules require.

Grievance Redressal

Raise a complaint about how your data is handled with the Privacy & Grievance Officer below.

Nomination

Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or are unable to do so yourself.

Contact the Privacy & Grievance Officer
